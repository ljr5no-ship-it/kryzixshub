The essentials.

What is Solana?

Solana is an open, decentralized blockchain designed for high-throughput applications — from payments and financial markets to games and consumer products.

How fast is the network?

Solana is engineered for up to 65,000 transactions per second, with block times measured in hundreds of milliseconds and a single composable global state.

How much does a transaction cost?

A standard transaction typically costs a fraction of a cent — often around $0.00025. Exact fees vary with transaction complexity and any optional priority fee.

What is Proof of History?

Proof of History is Solana's cryptographic clock — a verifiable sequence of events that lets validators agree on transaction ordering efficiently, while Proof of Stake secures consensus.

Who runs Solana?

Independent validators around the world verify transactions and secure the network. Anyone who meets the technical requirements can participate.
